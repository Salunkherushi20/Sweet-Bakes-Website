Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In brief: The current website does not send enquiry form details to Sweet Bakes or store them on a server. The form prepares a message in your browser for you to copy and send yourself.

Information you enter

The custom cake enquiry form may ask for your name, contact details, preferred cake, serving size, date and design notes. These details are processed in your browser to create a draft message. The website does not currently submit that information to a Sweet Bakes server or save it in a website database.

What happens when you copy the message

When you choose to copy the draft, your browser's clipboard feature is used. You decide where to paste and send it. Any information you share through another messaging, email or social platform is then subject to that platform's own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or account sign-in features. Hosting providers and your browser may process basic technical information as part of delivering the website.

External images and services

Some cake photographs are loaded from Unsplash. Your browser may connect to that service to display those images, under its own privacy practices.

Children

Please have a parent or guardian make enquiries on behalf of anyone under the age required to provide their own contact information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if website features change. If enquiries are later transmitted or stored online, this page should be revised to explain what is collected, why, how long it is kept and how to request deletion.
